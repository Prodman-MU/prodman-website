Design a visually engaging “Event Information” section for the Product Management Club website.

The section should present four events as a connected product-building journey:
Discover → Design → Validate → Showcase.

Begin with the heading “From Problem to Product” and the subheading:
“Four high-energy experiences designed to take you through the complete product-building journey—discover, design, validate, and launch.”

Display the events using a modern vertical timeline or four interactive cards. Each card must include:
- Event number
- Event title
- Date
- Internal or External badge
- One strong tagline
- A concise 2–3 line description
- Key learning outcomes
- A clear CTA button

Events:

1. Product Design Workshop & Hackathon
Date: 14 August 2026
Type: Internal Event
Tagline: “Bring a problem. Leave with a prototype.”
Description: Participants identify a meaningful problem, understand their users, generate ideas, and build a rapid prototype in one intensive product-design experience.
Outcomes: Problem discovery, design thinking, ideation, rapid prototyping
CTA: “Register Now”
Add an urgency badge: “Less than a week to go”

2. The Product Challenge
Date: 9 October 2026
Type: External Flagship Event
Tagline: “Solve beyond the classroom.”
Description: Participants tackle a real-world product challenge, uncover user insights, define a product strategy, and pitch a feasible and impactful solution.
Outcomes: User research, product strategy, prioritisation, solution pitching
CTA: “Coming Soon”

3. Build, Test & Iterate
Date: 4 December 2026
Type: Internal Event
Tagline: “Great products are not born finished—they evolve.”
Description: Teams test assumptions, gather feedback, measure outcomes, and improve their early solutions through rapid iterations.
Outcomes: Validation, user testing, metrics, product iteration
CTA: “Save the Date”

4. Product Showcase: The Grand Finale
Date: 5 February 2027
Type: External Grand Finale
Tagline: “From your first problem statement to a product worth presenting.”
Description: Teams present their refined products to industry leaders, mentors, and fellow builders, demonstrating the complete journey from problem discovery to final execution.
Outcomes: Product showcase, expert feedback, networking, recognition
CTA: “Save the Date”

Visual direction:
- Bold, youthful, product-and-technology-focused design
- Dark background with electric blue, purple, and neon green accents
- Connect all four events using a progress line
- Differentiate internal and external events with coloured badges
- Add subtle hover animations and product-related icons
- Highlight the nearest event with a countdown or urgency card
- Keep the layout clean, premium, responsive, and easy to scan
