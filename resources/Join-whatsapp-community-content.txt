Create a bold, high-conversion community section for the Product Management Club website.

The section should invite visitors to join the club’s WhatsApp community and subscribe to its newsletter. Make it feel exciting and exclusive—not like a generic website subscription form.

Main headline:
“Don’t Just Watch Products Evolve. Be Part of What’s Next.”

Supporting copy:
“Get event drops, product insights, AI trends, opportunities, resources, and the occasional hot take—delivered directly to you.”

Create two visually distinct cards:

CARD 1 — WHATSAPP COMMUNITY

Title:
“Join the Product Circle”

Tagline:
“Where product conversations continue after the event ends.”

Description:
“Get instant event updates, workshop announcements, opportunities, resources, and connect with people who are equally obsessed with building better products.”

Key highlights:
- Be the first to know about upcoming events
- Meet fellow builders and product enthusiasts
- Discover competitions, projects, and opportunities
- Exchange ideas, feedback, and useful resources

CTA button:
“Join the WhatsApp Community →”

Add a WhatsApp icon and a small line near the CTA:
“Less noise. More product.”

CARD 2 — NEWSLETTER

Title:
“Product Drops, Minus the Fluff”

Tagline:
“Sharp product thinking, delivered to your inbox.”

Description:
“A curated dose of product breakdowns, AI developments, useful frameworks, industry insights, opportunities, and everything worth knowing in the product world.”

Include:
- A clean email-address input field
- A bold subscription button reading “Send Me the Good Stuff →”
- A small privacy note: “No spam. No boring emails. Unsubscribe anytime.”

Optional newsletter topics displayed as small tags:
Product Teardowns | AI Trends | PM Frameworks | Opportunities | Club Updates

Closing statement across the bottom:
“Your next idea, opportunity, teammate, or breakthrough might be one update away.”

Visual direction:
- Use a modern split-screen or two-card layout
- Place WhatsApp on the left and Newsletter on the right
- Use a dark, premium technology-inspired background
- Add vibrant green accents to the WhatsApp card
- Use electric blue or purple accents for the newsletter card
- Include subtle gradients, soft glows, and hover animations
- Add playful product-related microcopy
- Keep the design youthful, bold, minimal, and responsive
- Make both CTA buttons highly visible
- On mobile, stack the two cards vertically
- Avoid excessive text and maintain strong visual hierarchy
